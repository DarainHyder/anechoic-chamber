# Tester recruitment post

**Before posting:** read each community's rules and ask the moderators first. Many health communities restrict recruitment posts. Replace the text in square brackets.

**Keep track of people:** give each tester a code (T01, T02 and so on) and add a row to `private/testers/tester_log.csv`, copied from `tester_log_template.csv`. Keep their contact details in a separate file, `private/testers/contacts.txt`. Mark someone `booked` only once they've returned the consent note and intake form.

Check your progress at any time with:

```sh
python -m scripts.check_testers
```

Filters like this can leave strange leftover sounds, and those can be triggers too. Only people who live with misophonia can tell me if it really helps, so I'm looking for 5 to 10 volunteers.

**What it involves**

- A short form now about your main triggers, and any sounds you never want to hear in a test.
- One listening session in about three months, at home with your own headphones, lasting about 30 minutes. You'd rate pairs of short clips.

**Please know before you sign up**

- The clips contain trigger sounds, so some may be uncomfortable. You can stop at any moment, for any reason.
- You must be 18 or over.
- This is unpaid, and it's not a treatment or a diagnosis.
- Your ratings are stored under a code, not your name.
