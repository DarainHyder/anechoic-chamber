**Project:** Anechoic Chamber, a free tool that removes trigger sounds such as chewing and sniffing from audio.

**Who is running this:** [YOUR NAME], [YOUR CONTACT DETAILS]

**What you'll do:** fill in a short form about your triggers now, then join one listening session in about three months. In the session you'll listen at home, on your own headphones, to about 20 pairs of short clips and rate each one. It takes about 30 minutes.

**Risks:** the clips contain trigger sounds, and some versions may contain leftover sounds from the filter. They may cause discomfort, anger, anxiety or distress. Please:

- Tell me about any sounds you never want to hear, and I'll leave them out.
- Keep the volume low and comfortable.
- Stop whenever you want. A stop button is always available, and you don't need to explain why.

**This isn't treatment:** the study doesn't diagnose or treat misophonia. If sounds or this study cause you serious distress, please stop and speak to a doctor or mental health professional. If you ever feel unsafe, contact your local emergency services.

**Your data:**

- Your ratings and form answers are stored under a code, such as T01, not your name.
- Your contact details are kept in a separate private file, used only to arrange the session, and deleted when testing ends.
- Combined, anonymous results may be published in a public evaluation report.
- No audio is recorded from you.

**Your choices:** taking part is voluntary and unpaid. You can withdraw at any time, before or after the session, by contacting me. I'll delete your answers. Results that have already been published in combined form can't be changed.
