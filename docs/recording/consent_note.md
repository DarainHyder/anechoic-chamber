## English

**Project:** Anechoic Chamber is a free tool being built to help people with misophonia, a condition where everyday sounds such as chewing cause strong distress. It removes those sounds from audio.

**Who is asking:** [YOUR NAME], [YOUR CONTACT DETAILS]

**What I'm asking:** to record sounds you make, such as eating, sniffing, clicking a pen or typing. Your voice may be heard in some clips.

**What happens to the recordings:**

- They are used to train and test the AI model.
- They are stored in a private folder on my computer and in a private online dataset on Kaggle, a machine learning website, so that the model can be trained. They are not made public.
- Your name is not stored with them. You get a code instead.
- Short clips may be played to a small group of volunteer testers, who rate how well the tool works.
- The finished AI model may be shared publicly. It does not contain your recordings.

**Your choices:**

- Taking part is voluntary, and you can say no without giving a reason.
- You can stop at any time and ask me to delete your recordings. I will delete them and leave them out of any future training. A model that has already been trained can't be changed, but the recordings won't be used again.

**Tick each one you agree to:**

- [ ] You may record me and use the recordings to train and test the model.
- [ ] You may keep the recordings in a private Kaggle dataset.
- [ ] Volunteer testers may hear clips that include me.
- [ ] Clips may include my voice.
